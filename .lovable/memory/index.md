- `/portfolio` — Portfolio & Offerings (9 sub-tabs: Portfolio, Competitive, Positioning, Pricing Strategy, Launch Planning, Collateral, Social Strategy, Innovation, Recommendations)

- `/content` — Content Creation (Templates + Recent Content)
- `/leads` — Lead Generation (Campaigns, Lead DB, Funnel, Optimization)
- `/crm` — CRM (Pipeline, Automations, Activity Feed)
- `/pre-fortitude` — Pre-Fortitude AI (Validation layer: experiments, pilots, PMF)
